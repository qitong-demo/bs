## 一、改后全文（可直接整段替换）

> As the platform and digital economy grow, food delivery riders, couriers and ride-hailing drivers form an important part of the labor force, yet they still struggle to access rest facilities, public services, policy information and complaint channels. Friendly stations exist for them, but most rely on offline registration and manual management, leaving information scattered, reservations inconvenient and appeals slow.

> This thesis designs and implements a friendly station service and appeal response platform based on SpringBoot and Vue, with separated front and back ends: SpringBoot provides RESTful interfaces, Vue builds the user and administrator pages, and MySQL stores business data. It serves ordinary users, station administrators and platform administrators, and covers authentication, station management, service reservation, appeal handling, policy information and announcements, activity registration, service evaluation and statistics.

> Users can query stations, reserve services, submit appeals, follow progress, read policy information, register for activities and evaluate services online; administrators can maintain station resources, review reservations, handle appeals, publish information and activities, and view statistics. Reservations and appeals run as a closed loop, improving information transparency and management efficiency.

> Tests show that the main functions operate correctly and the core business processes are completed as expected. The platform gives workers in new forms of employment a convenient service channel and offers a reference for building grassroots public service platforms digitally.

> **Key words:** workers in new forms of employment; friendly station; appeal response; service reservation; SpringBoot; Vue

## 三、压缩手法（都是删冗余，不是删信息）

| 手法 | 原文 | 改后 |
|---|---|---|
| 并列句改主谓并列 | "the system adopts a…, uses…, Vue to build…, and MySQL to store…" | "SpringBoot provides…, Vue builds…, and MySQL stores…" |
| 两句合一，去掉过渡词 | "…part of the labor force. **However, they still face difficulties in accessing** rest facilities…" | "…part of the labor force, **yet they still struggle to access** rest facilities…" |
| 去掉模板式开头 | "**To address these problems, this thesis** designs and implements…" | "This thesis designs and implements…" |
| 名词化改动词 | "have become an important part of" | "form an important part of" |
| 去掉冗余限定 | "query station **information**""**operational** statistics""**processing** progress" | "query stations""statistics""progress" |
| 从句改介词短语 | "which may result in scattered information" | "leaving information scattered" |
| 合并同义结构 | "provides a convenient service channel for workers… and offers a reference for the digital construction of…" | "gives workers… a convenient service channel and offers a reference for building… digitally" |

另外，这一版顺带**破掉了 3 个容易被 AIGC 检测器抓的模板特征**（原英文摘要 3 句都在这份检测报告里被判为高度疑似，96.9% / 69.2% / 90.7%）：

- "With the rapid development of the…" 这类套话开头 → 改成 "As the platform and digital economy grow"
- "However, they still face difficulties in…" 这类转折模板 → 并入上句
- "To address these problems, this thesis…" 这类承接模板 → 直接陈述
- 原来的"主—谓—宾"完全对称长句（三句全是 25—40 词的等长结构）→ 改成 8—24 词的长短交错

## 五、两点说明

### 5.1 为什么止步在 −18%

英文摘要的篇幅主要由**并列项**构成：9 项功能、7 项用户操作、5 项管理操作、4 类困难，
这些并列项加起来占了全文一半以上。删掉任何一项都属于改变语义，因此继续压缩只能靠删内容，
与"不改变语义"的要求冲突。这一版压掉的是**句式冗余和过渡词**，信息点全部保留。

### 5.2 如果还需要更短，建议中英文同步压

现状对应关系：

| | 篇幅 |
|---|---|
| 中文摘要正文 | 758 字（4 段：202 / 222 / 201 / 133） |
| 英文摘要正文 | 216 词（改后） |
| 词/字比 | 约 0.285（正常区间） |

英中比在正常范围内。若要把英文压到 170 词以下，**中文摘要需同步压缩**（建议从 758 字压到约 600 字），
否则中英文摘要内容会对不上——评审通常会对照检查这一点。
